# Last Train Home

**Level 2 — three-shot character sequence**

A woman waits on a rainy night platform, turns to the arriving train, and steps aboard.

## Prompt

```text
Use video-production to create a 12–15 second cinematic three-shot sequence called "Last Train Home".

A woman waits alone on a railway platform at night in light rain. A train arrives, she looks toward it, and the sequence ends as she steps aboard.

The same character must remain recognisably identical across every shot.

Requirements:
- Duration: 12–15 seconds
- Format: 16:9
- Tone: intimate, cinematic, slightly melancholic
- Character: one woman in a dark coat carrying a small overnight bag
- Environment: wet railway platform at night
- Lighting: practical station lights reflected in wet surfaces
- Weather: light rain throughout
- Train: visually consistent between shots
- Dialogue: none

Plan approximately three shots:
1. Wide establishing shot: woman alone on the platform as the train approaches
2. Medium portrait: she turns toward the arriving train
3. Wider moving shot: she walks toward an open carriage door

Workflow:
- Establish visual direction
- Create a consistent character reference
- Build a three-frame storyboard before video generation
- Create a shot plan defining framing, action, camera movement, duration, and continuity requirements
- Approve reference frames before generating final motion
- Generate alternatives only where useful
- Select the strongest take for each shot
- Assemble the selected shots into an edit
- Evaluate continuity, pacing, identity stability, and technical quality

If one shot fails, diagnose and regenerate only the smallest affected production unit. Do not recreate approved shots unnecessarily.

Optimise for:
- character identity across shots
- believable rain and wet-surface reflections
- coherent screen direction
- restrained acting
- cinematic pacing
```
