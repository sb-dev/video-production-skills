## Notes carried downstream

- Both selected sheets share a neutral grey seamless backdrop and even lighting, so
  neither imports a competing lighting direction into shot reference frames.
- Walk direction inside the sheets is **not** a continuity constraint. Screen direction
  is imposed per shot by the shot plan (Elias L→R, Nora R→L).
- Nora's bun reads most strongly in three-quarter and rear views — favour those angles
  when she must be identified from behind (SH03).
