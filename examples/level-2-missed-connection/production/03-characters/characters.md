# Character References — "Missed Connection"

- **lifecycleState:** final
- **decisionState:** approved
- **parent:** `02-visual-direction/visual-direction.md`

Two people, two silhouettes, two protected colours. Designed so a viewer can
re-identify each of them at wide-shot scale from **colour + hair silhouette alone**,
before any facial detail is available.

---

## CHAR-M — "ELIAS"

**Canonical description (paste verbatim into every downstream prompt):**

> a tall lean man in his early thirties with dark brown tousled hair and short stubble,
> warm olive skin, wearing an unbuttoned rust-orange corduroy jacket over a charcoal
> crewneck sweater, dark charcoal trousers and brown leather boots, a dark brown leather
> satchel worn cross-body, holding a phone in his right hand

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Age / build | early 30s, ~185 cm, lean |
| Hair | dark brown, tousled, slightly overgrown; short stubble beard |
| Skin / eyes | warm olive; brown eyes |
| **Protected colour** | **rust / burnt orange** (corduroy jacket) |
| Wardrobe | rust-orange corduroy jacket, charcoal crewneck, charcoal trousers, brown boots |
| Props | dark brown leather cross-body satchel; phone in right hand |
| Silhouette cue | tall, long stride, bag strap diagonal across chest |
| Demeanour | preoccupied, slightly late, eyes down or ahead — never scanning |
