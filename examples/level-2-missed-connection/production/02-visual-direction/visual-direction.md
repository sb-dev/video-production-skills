# Visual Direction — "Missed Connection"

- **lifecycleState:** final
- **decisionState:** approved
- **parent:** `01-brief/brief.md`

Everything below is a **carry-down constraint**. Downstream artifacts inherit it and
may not silently reopen it.

## 1. Register

Observational photoreal cinema. The camera behaves like a documentary operator who
found a good position and mostly stayed there. It does not chase, it does not
editorialise. The romance is in the geometry, not in the lighting.

**Reference register:** naturalistic European station photography — available light,
long lens compression, crowd as texture. *Not* commercial, *not* glossy, *not* teal-orange
graded to the point of stylisation.

> **Selection bias for reference frames:** when comparing candidates, prefer the one that
> looks *photographed* over the one that is *lit most dramatically*. Drama here comes from
> staging.

## 2. Lighting

| Source | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Roof glass | Cool blue-grey dusk, ~18:00, falling off fast. Top light, soft. |
| Concourse pendants | Warm sodium/amber, hanging in rows, visible in frame as practicals. |
| Departure board | Cool white/amber LED spill, acts as a soft key on anyone facing it. |
| Kiosks & shopfronts | Warm amber pools at floor level, screen-left of the board. |

Net palette: **cool overhead, warm at human height.** The crowd reads as silhouette-ish
mid-tones; the two leads are separated by wardrobe colour, not by special lighting.

Mild practical halation on lamps. Fine natural film grain. Gentle highlight rolloff.
No lens flares, no volumetric god-ray theatrics, no rain.

## 3. Lens and camera language

| Shot | Lens | Movement |
|---|---|---|
| SH01 wide | ~28–35 mm equivalent, deep-ish focus | almost imperceptible slow push-in |
| SH02 crossing | ~85 mm, compressed, shallow | locked off |
| SH03 glance back | ~50 mm | very slight tracking, then settle |

Handheld micro-motion only. No whip pans, no crane moves, no dolly zooms.

## 4. Grade

Desaturated base with two protected colours: **rust-orange** and **deep teal**.
Blacks lifted slightly, milky. Contrast medium-low. Slight warm bias in the shadows.

## 5. Station geography — the map

This is the single most important continuity artifact. All three shots are staged on
**one** concourse, and the camera never crosses the concourse long axis.

```text
                        N  (platform gates 7–12, under the arched trainshed)
        ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │  ▓▓▓ gates ▓▓▓        ▓▓▓ gates ▓▓▓        ▓▓▓ gates ▓▓▓     │
        │                                                             │
        │        ┌───────────────────────┐                            │
        │        │  DEPARTURE BOARD      │   ← hangs above concourse   │
        │        │  ( ○ clock beneath )  │      centre-north           │
        │        └───────────────────────┘                            │
        │                                                             │
   W    │   ☕ kiosk           ▌ STONE COLUMN ▐            ⌂ arch      │   E
        │   (warm amber)      (mid-concourse)          (side hall)     │
        │                                                             │
        │  ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← NORA'S FLOW ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ←  │
        │  → → → → → → → → → → ELIAS'S FLOW → → → → → → → → → → → → →  │
        └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                        S   ▲ ▲ ▲  CAMERA IS ALWAYS HERE  ▲ ▲ ▲
                            (south side, looking north)
```

### Fixed landmarks (must recur, at least one per shot)

| ID | Landmark | Function |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Large departure board, dark panel, amber/white listings | Primary anchor. Visible SH01, SH02, SH03. |
| L2 | Round station clock beneath the board, reading **17:48** | Time anchor. Same time all three shots. |
| L3 | Arched steel-and-glass trainshed roof, rows of pendant lamps | Ceiling signature, proves same hall. |
| L4 | Cylindrical stone column, mid-concourse | Foreground element in SH02, background-left in SH01. |
| L5 | Coffee kiosk, warm amber, screen-left of the board | Warm pool, visible SH01 and SH03. |
| L6 | Platform gates numbered 7–12 along the north side | Direction-of-travel destination for Elias. |

### The 180° line

The concourse long axis (E–W) is the line. **The camera stays south of it in every shot.**
Consequence: the departure board (L1) and clock (L2) are always in the upper half of frame,
and the platform gates are always beyond the crowd, never behind camera.

## 6. Screen direction — the spine of the piece

This is not a style preference. It is the mechanism by which the story is legible.

| Character | Vector | Never |
|---|---|---|
| **ELIAS** (man) | moves **screen LEFT → RIGHT** in every shot | never right-to-left |
| **NORA** (woman) | moves **screen RIGHT → LEFT** in every shot | never left-to-right |

Opposing vectors on a shared axis = the audience reads *converging paths* without being
told. Because the camera never crosses the line, the vectors stay true across cuts, and
SH02 can put them in one frame without contradicting SH01.

**Eyeline rule at the crossing (SH02):**

- Elias's eyeline goes **down-and-right** (phone, then toward the platform gates).
- Nora's eyeline goes **up-and-left** (toward the departure board L1).

Their sightlines are near-perpendicular and diverge. They are ~2 metres apart and
neither look could physically land on the other. That is the whole point, and it must
be constructed, not hoped for.

**Eyeline rule at the glance back (SH03):**

- Nora, still moving right-to-left, turns her head back over her **right** shoulder,
  looking **screen-right** — the exact direction Elias travelled and has now gone.
- The audience already knows what is (no longer) there. She does not.

## 7. Crowd

Density: heavy but walkable — evening rush, people flowing in both directions, some
standing still reading the board. Crowd wardrobe is **muted only**: charcoal, black,
navy, beige, olive, grey. No red, no orange, no teal on background performers — those
two colours are reserved for the leads so they stay findable at wide-shot scale.

Motion blur on crowd is welcome; it separates the leads by relative sharpness.

## 8. Audio direction

No dialogue. No music. Ambient station bed only:

- broad crowd murmur, non-intelligible
- rolling suitcase wheels on stone
- distant train brake squeal and departure rumble
- a low, unintelligible tannoy announcement — indistinct, never a comprehensible word
- footsteps, layered

Audio is built **once for the whole master**, not per shot, so it does not cut with picture.

## 9. Negative constraints

No dialogue. No subtitles or on-screen text. No readable signage text beyond generic
station typography. No modern logos or brands. No smiling-at-each-other. No slow-motion.
No rack-focus stunts. No lens flares. No rain. No music.
