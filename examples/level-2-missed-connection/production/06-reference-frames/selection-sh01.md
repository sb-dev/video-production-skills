# SH01 Reference Frame — selection

- **decisionState:** selected → approved
- **selected:** `sh01-a` → `selected/SH01-wide.jpg`
- **provenance:** `jobs-sh01-v1.provenance.json`

SH01 is not just one shot's target — it is the **environment lock** for the whole
sequence. It is selected on geographic usability first and photographic quality second.

## Candidate assessment

| | Geography chain (west → east) | Vectors | Photographic | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **sh01-a** | kiosk · board · column | Elias in clean right-facing profile with clear floor; Nora legible, slightly camera-facing | cool, neutral, well motion-blurred crowd, genuine high-wide vantage | **SELECTED** |
| sh01-b | kiosk · column · **board** | best of the three; clock legibly reads 17:48 | strongest image — warm floor pools against cold roof, real depth | **rejected — geography** |
| sh01-c | kiosk · board · column | both leads face camera; vectors weak | flatter, lower vantage, crowd under-blurred, "COFFEE" signage legible | rejected |

## Why the best-looking frame was rejected

`sh01-b` is the strongest photograph of the three. It is unusable anyway.

It places the departure board **east of the stone column**. Every downstream shot then
inherits that order. In SH02 the column is a foreground element at the frame edge, so an
east-of-column board must appear **upper-right** — which forces Nora to look **up-and-right**
to read it. Elias is already travelling and looking right. Both eyelines would then point
the same way, and a woman looking right while the man exits right reads as *she sees him*.

That inverts the entire premise. The eyeline divergence in SH02 is the mechanism of the
story, and it is only constructible if the board sits **west** of the column.

`sh01-a` and `sh01-c` both put the board west of the column, preserving the chain
**kiosk → board → column** and keeping a left-looking Nora available in SH02.

## Why sh01-a over sh01-c

- Elias is in clean right-facing profile with a pocket of clear floor. In `sh01-c` both
  leads face camera, so neither vector reads and the convergence premise is lost.
- Genuine high-wide mezzanine vantage; `sh01-c` sits low and flat and reads as a snapshot.
- Crowd motion blur separates the two sharp leads from the anonymous flow.
- No legible signage text, per the visual direction.

## Accepted defects

| Defect | Accepted because |
|---|---|
| Clock face is illegible rather than reading 17:48 | At this scale no viewer resolves it. An illegible clock is *safer* than a legible one — a legible 17:48 in SH01 would have to be matched exactly in SH02 and SH03 or it becomes a visible continuity break. Non-legible in all three shots is the cheaper guarantee. |
| Two or three small red garments deep in the background crowd | Sub-1% of frame area, heavily motion-blurred, and far from both leads. Does not compete with the rust-orange foreground. Correcting it would mean regenerating and re-risking the geography. |
| Nora is slightly camera-facing rather than in clean left profile | She is a **first frame**, not the finished shot. Her leftward vector is imposed by the motion prompt in stage 07. |

## Ledger correction carried forward

The shot plan predicted the board at *upper centre* in SH01. As generated it sits
**upper centre-left, west of the column**. Ledger updated; the westward camera migration
(SH01 centre-left → SH02 left → SH03 right) still holds and stays monotonic.
