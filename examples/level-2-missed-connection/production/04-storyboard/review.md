## Correction 1 — kiosk/board relationship inverted in SB-03

**Found:** SB-01 places the coffee kiosk (L5) screen-**left** of the departure board (L1).
SB-03 places the kiosk screen-**right** of it. Same camera side, contradictory geography —
this is exactly the kind of error that makes a viewer feel, without being able to say why,
that these are two different buildings.

**Resolution:** L5 is west of L1 on the map, and the camera never crosses the concourse
axis, so **the kiosk is always screen-left of the board**. In SH03 Nora is walking west,
past and beyond the board, so the correct arrangement is:

- departure board + clock → upper **right** of frame (behind her, to the east)
- coffee kiosk warm pool → screen **left** (ahead of her, to the west)

This is now a hard constraint in the shot plan. It also improves the shot: she glances
back toward the board side, which is the same side Elias exited.

## Correction 2 — the crossing frame is a *pre*-crossing frame

**Found:** SB-02 draws the moment *after* Elias has passed Nora — he is already frame-right
of her. Correct as a storyboard beat, wrong as a production input. The reference frame
becomes the video model's **first frame**, so drawing the post-crossing state would mean
the near-miss happens before the shot starts.

**Resolution:** SH02's reference frame must depict the **entry state** — Elias just
entering at the frame-left edge in the foreground, Nora already established mid-ground
right-of-centre. The crossing then occurs inside the generated motion at ~2.6 s.

Same correction applies to SH01 (Elias entering bottom-left, not mid-frame) and SH03
(Nora walking away, head still forward — the turn happens inside the shot).

## Not changed
