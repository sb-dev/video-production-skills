# Atlas One

**Level 5 — adventure e-bike campaign**

Adventure hero film, engineering film, vertical social cut, and bumper for a rugged e-bike.

## Prompt

```text
Use video-production to direct a launch campaign for a fictional premium adventure e-bike called "Atlas One".

Use approved Advertising, Narrative, product, and Music artifacts when supplied. Video owns their visual execution, not their upstream strategic or musical decisions.

Deliver:

1. 45-second hero adventure film
2. 20-second product/engineering film
3. 15-second vertical social cut
4. 6-second bumper
5. required delivery variants

Product:
- rugged premium electric bicycle
- graphite frame
- distinctive integrated battery profile
- orange suspension detail
- recognisable headlight

Campaign tone:
freedom, engineering, distance, capability

Locations may include:
- early-morning city departure
- forest trail
- mountain road
- exposed viewpoint

Workflow:
- Establish a canonical bike reference and manifest
- Lock rider and wardrobe references where recurring
- Define campaign-wide video visual direction from approved upstream inputs
- Plan reusable location/product assets
- Storyboard hero film across several environments
- Resolve difficult riding/action sequences with animatics or motion prototypes before final generation
- Generate and select shots
- Preserve bicycle geometry throughout motion
- Build rough and fine cuts
- Finish hero film
- Produce a more engineering-focused product film
- Produce social and bumper versions deliberately rather than merely cropping
- Integrate approved music and program audio
- Evaluate continuity, product fidelity, action realism, pacing, message execution, and deliverable QC

Optimise for:
- bicycle fidelity during action
- rider and equipment continuity
- convincing speed and terrain interaction
- environmental scale
- coherent visual identity across all campaign videos
```
