# Helios

**Level 5 — home robot campaign**

Brand hero, product demo, lifestyle social, and bumper for a calm domestic robot.

## Prompt

```text
Use video-production to direct a multi-video launch campaign for a fictional home robot called "Helios".

Where upstream campaign strategy, narrative concepts, product references, dialogue, or music are provided, consume those artifacts rather than replacing their domain decisions.

Deliver:

1. 45-second brand hero film
2. 25-second product demonstration
3. 15-second family/lifestyle social film
4. 6-second launch bumper
5. required vertical adaptations

Product:
- compact domestic robot
- soft white shell
- dark expressive display
- wheeled base
- recognisable proportions and movement language

Campaign goal:
Present Helios as useful, calm, and naturally integrated into everyday domestic life.

Possible scenes:
- morning kitchen
- remembering household tasks
- carrying or retrieving an object
- interacting with family members
- quiet evening charging
- product beauty/detail shots

Workflow:
- Lock shared robot/product references
- Establish domestic visual direction
- Preserve approved personality/narrative boundaries without inventing new campaign positioning
- Plan reusable environments and human references
- Storyboard hero film
- Test difficult robot-human interactions with reference frames or motion prototypes
- Generate and select final shots
- Assemble and evaluate the hero film
- Create a functionally clearer product demonstration
- Produce lifestyle social and bumper assets
- Integrate approved music, dialogue, SFX, and graphics
- Finish and QC each master/delivery

Optimise for:
- robot identity and scale
- believable physical interaction
- consistency across different rooms
- natural human reactions
- distinction between emotional brand film and functional product demo
```
