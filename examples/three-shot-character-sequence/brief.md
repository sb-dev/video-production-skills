# Brief

Produce a 10–12 second, three-shot cinematic sequence.

A fictional bicycle courier arrives at a quiet apartment door, notices a handwritten note taped beside the handle, reads it, and relaxes visibly.

## Requirements

- The same courier must remain recognisable across all three shots.
- Naturalistic morning interior light.
- Restrained handheld camera language.
- No dialogue.
- Final output: one 16:9 video master suitable for review.
- The emotional progression must read without explanatory text.
