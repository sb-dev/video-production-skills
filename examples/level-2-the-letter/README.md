# The Letter

**Level 2 — restrained prop drama**

A woman opens a handwritten letter beside a window and reacts quietly.

## Prompt

```text
Use video-production to create a 12–15 second dramatic three-shot sequence called "The Letter".

A woman sitting beside a window opens a handwritten letter, reads a few lines, and reacts quietly before looking outside.

The emotion should come from performance, framing, and editing rather than exaggerated acting.

Requirements:
- Duration: 12–15 seconds
- Format: 16:9
- Tone: restrained, intimate drama
- Character: one woman, identity preserved throughout
- Location: quiet apartment beside a large window
- Prop: one recognisable handwritten letter
- Lighting: soft natural daylight
- Dialogue: none

Plan approximately three shots:
1. Medium shot of her opening the envelope
2. Insert/close shot of hands and letter
3. Close portrait as she reacts and turns toward the window

Workflow:
- Establish character, room, and lighting references
- Storyboard the sequence
- Create a shot plan covering eyelines, hand continuity, letter position, and timing
- Approve reference frames
- Generate shot candidates
- Assemble and evaluate the edit

Optimise for:
- subtle facial performance
- convincing hands and prop interaction
- continuity of the letter
- natural shot progression
- restrained editorial pacing
```
