# The Red Umbrella

**Level 2 — colour-anchor tracking sequence**

One red umbrella stays identical across three shots of a muted, wet grey city.

## Prompt

```text
Use video-production to create a 12–15 second cinematic sequence called "The Red Umbrella".

Follow one person carrying a vivid red umbrella through an otherwise muted grey city after heavy rain.

The red umbrella is the persistent visual anchor and must remain identical across the sequence.

Requirements:
- Duration: 12–15 seconds
- Format: 16:9
- Tone: graphic, cinematic, elegant
- Palette: predominantly grey, blue-grey, concrete, wet asphalt
- Persistent accent: one saturated red umbrella
- Character: same person and wardrobe throughout
- Weather: rain has mostly stopped, surfaces remain wet
- Dialogue: none

Plan approximately three shots:
1. High or wide establishing shot revealing the red umbrella among muted commuters
2. Lateral tracking shot following the character across a wet street
3. Rear or three-quarter shot as the character disappears into the city

Workflow:
- Lock the visual palette first
- Establish the umbrella and character references
- Storyboard the colour composition of all shots
- Produce shot-specific reference frames
- Generate and select video candidates
- Assemble the edit
- Evaluate the umbrella, wardrobe, colour palette, direction of travel, and environmental continuity

Optimise for:
- exact persistence of the red umbrella
- strong colour composition
- wet-city atmosphere
- controlled camera movement
- visual continuity
```
