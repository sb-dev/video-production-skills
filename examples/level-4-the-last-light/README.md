# The Last Light

**Level 4 — atmospheric storm short**

A lighthouse keeper prepares for a storm until the beam cuts through the weather.

## Prompt

```text
Use video-production to create a 35–45 second atmospheric short called "The Last Light".

Premise:
A lighthouse keeper prepares for an approaching storm. As visibility disappears, the lighthouse beam activates and cuts through the weather.

Requirements:
- Duration: 35–45 seconds
- Format: 16:9
- Tone: atmospheric, isolated, dramatic
- Character: one lighthouse keeper
- Persistent location: one recognisable lighthouse
- Environments:
  - lighthouse interior
  - lantern room
  - exterior cliff/coast
- Weather must progress consistently from threatening to severe
- Audio: wind, sea, machinery, distant thunder, minimal score
- Dialogue: none

Sequences:

1. Storm Approaches
Exterior and interior preparation.

2. Inside the Lighthouse
Keeper activates equipment as weather worsens.

3. The Beam
The lighthouse comes alive as the storm reaches full intensity.

Workflow:
- Establish lighthouse, keeper, costume, weather, and colour references
- Storyboard environmental progression
- Build shot plans around weather and geographic continuity
- Use animatic where useful
- Approve key reference frames
- Generate sequences independently while inheriting shared references
- Build rough and fine cuts
- Evaluate whether weather escalation and dramatic structure read clearly
- Add sound and finishing
- Produce final master and QC

Optimise for:
- environmental continuity
- progressive weather
- scale
- natural dramatic lighting
- restrained human performance
```
