# Nova

**Level 5 — AR glasses campaign**

Hero, demo, social, and bumper films for lightweight titanium AR glasses with restrained interface graphics.

## Prompt

```text
Use video-production to direct a launch campaign for fictional premium augmented-reality glasses called "Nova".

Where upstream Advertising, Narrative, UI, or Music artifacts exist, treat them as approved production inputs.

Create:

1. 45-second hero film
2. 20-second product demonstration
3. 15-second social video
4. 6-second bumper
5. 9:16 variants where appropriate

Product:
- lightweight dark titanium AR glasses
- minimal frame
- recognisable temple geometry
- no bulky headset appearance

Campaign idea:
Digital information appears naturally within everyday life without isolating the wearer from the physical world.

Video production may include:
- product macro photography
- wearer interaction
- urban navigation
- translation/information scenarios
- restrained interface graphics
- lifestyle scenes
- final hero reveal

Workflow:
- Preserve approved product and campaign artifacts
- Establish shared visual and interface direction for video
- Create product manifest and wearer references
- Plan graphics/interface integration before final shots
- Storyboard the hero film
- Generate reusable reference frames
- Produce shots with consistent product geometry and human interaction
- Assemble and evaluate hero film
- Produce product-demo video with greater functional clarity
- Produce social and bumper versions for their own communication goals
- Finish masters and delivery variants
- Evaluate product fidelity, interface legibility, continuity, message clarity, pacing, and QC

Do not allow graphics to become generic floating holograms if the approved interface language says otherwise.

Optimise for:
- product consistency on faces
- believable human-device interaction
- clean graphics integration
- coherent campaign identity
- useful differentiation between hero and demo content
```
