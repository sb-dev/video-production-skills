# Visual Direction — Luxury Watch Hero Shot

## Output
6s, 16:9, single shot, luxury commercial product hero.

## Subject
One black mechanical wristwatch, resting (not worn) on a brushed metal surface.
- Case: polished black (PVD/DLC-style finish), sharp specular highlights, no dust/fingerprints
- Dial: dark, detailed — visible indices, hands (hour/minute/seconds), possibly a sub-dial, crisp legibility
- Strap: black, visible texture (leather grain or rubber ribbing), resting in soft coiled arrangement
- Crystal: subtle correct reflections, no blown-out glare obscuring dial

## Environment
- Surface: brushed metal (fine linear texture, cool grey), watch laid diagonally or slightly angled for dimension
- Background: near-black, minimal, soft gradient falloff — no props, no distracting elements

## Lighting
- Dark studio / low-key
- One narrow, directional highlight (softbox strip or gobo-shaped) that sweeps slowly across the case and dial over the duration — this is the primary motion driver for "elegance"
- Rim/edge light optional to separate case from background
- No flicker, no strobing — sweep must read as one continuous, controlled pass

## Camera
- Macro lens feel, shallow depth of field, dial in critical focus
- Slow push-in (dolly-in) combined with a subtle lateral drift (arc, not a straight slide) — creates parallax without disorientation
- No handheld shake, no whip pans, no rack focus pulls beyond a very gentle breathing depth

## Color / Tone
- Desaturated, cool-neutral palette (blacks, graphite, cold steel highlights)
- High contrast, deep blacks, controlled specular blooms (not clipped)
- Overall mood: precision, restraint, expense — think watch-house commercial (Rolex/IWC-style darkroom hero shots)

## Negative direction (avoid)
- No chaotic camera movement
- No busy background/props/hands/text overlays
- No warm/gold color cast unless case hardware calls for it
- No motion blur artifacts on dial (must stay readable throughout)
- No plastic-looking case material — must read as metal/ceramic

## Success criteria
- Dial details (indices, hands) remain sharp and legible across the full 6s
- Highlight sweep looks intentional and smooth, not glitchy
- Reflections are physically plausible, not noisy/artifacted
- Camera motion is smooth, no jitter, no warping of watch geometry
- Overall material fidelity reads as premium/expensive
