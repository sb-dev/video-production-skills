# Arc One

**Level 3 — wireless headphones**

Matte-black headphones with a continuous aluminium headband, from macro detail to final lock-up.

## Prompt

```text
Use video-production to create a 15-second launch commercial for fictional premium wireless headphones called "Arc One".

Requirements:
- Duration: 15 seconds
- Master: 16:9
- Delivery: 9:16 social version
- Tone: modern, minimal, precise
- Product: matte-black over-ear headphones with a distinctive continuous aluminium headband
- Persistent product identity across every shot
- Audio: detailed tactile sound design plus restrained electronic music
- Graphics: minimal "ARC ONE" title and final product lock-up

Sequence should combine:
- macro product details
- one rotating or moving hero product shot
- one human-use shot
- final graphic/product composition

Workflow:
- Create a product manifest
- Establish art direction and lighting language
- Storyboard product and human interaction shots
- Approve product reference frames before motion
- Generate and select candidates
- Assemble a precise commercial edit
- Integrate sound design and music
- Produce master and vertical adaptation
- Evaluate geometry, materials, human-product interaction, audio timing, and product consistency

Optimise for:
- exact headphone geometry
- premium material rendering
- smooth motion
- sound-to-picture synchronisation
- clean contemporary design
```
