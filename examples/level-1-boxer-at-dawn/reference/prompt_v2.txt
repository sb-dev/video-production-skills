A tight cinematic MEDIUM SHOT of a single male boxer training on a hanging heavy bag in a dim, empty boxing gym at dawn.

FRAMING — this is the most important requirement. This is a medium shot, not a wide shot. The boxer's torso fills most of the frame height. The frame is cropped from just above the top of his head down to mid-thigh. His feet and the floor beneath him are NOT visible. The camera is at chest level, angled very slightly upward. He occupies the left half of the frame; the black leather heavy bag fills the right edge of the frame, large and close, cut off by the right side of the frame.

The boxer stands close to the bag, well inside punching range — his lead glove is only a few centimetres from the bag's leather surface, almost touching it. He is lean and conditioned, bare-torsoed, wearing plain black training shorts and worn black leather boxing gloves over white hand wraps. He is in an orthodox stance, hips coiled and beginning to rotate, lead shoulder dropping, rear hand cocked at his jaw and just beginning to travel forward — the instant before the punch lands, not the impact itself. His jaw is set, his expression is focused, he is breathing hard from effort. Both hands are clearly visible and correctly formed inside the gloves. Anatomy is exact: two arms, two hands, correct elbow and shoulder articulation.

Sweat sheets across his shoulders, deltoids, chest and forearms, catching the light in hard specular highlights. Fine white chalk dust clings to his wraps and hangs in the air in a thin cloud between his gloves and the bag.

The gym is bare and uncluttered: raw grey concrete, one heavy bag on a steel chain, and deep empty darkness behind him. Pale cold dawn light glows faintly in the far background. There is nothing else in frame — no other equipment, no visible light fixtures, no other people, no signage.

Lighting is low-key and high contrast: one hard directional key from camera left and slightly behind, cold blue-grey dawn daylight raking across his shoulder, arm, ribs and cheekbone, and skimming the near face of the heavy bag, with dust motes suspended in the beam. Everything else falls away into deep shadow and near-black. This is natural photographic light only.

Shot on a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field with the boxer in critical focus. Desaturated cool-neutral colour grade — concrete grey and cold blue with warm skin as the only saturated element — deep rich blacks, controlled highlight roll-off on the sweat, fine organic film grain. Gritty but polished modern sports cinematography, documentary boxing photography.

Strictly avoid: any glowing outline, rim glow, neon edge light, energy effect, light streak or digital glow around the body or arms; any text, lettering, numbers, logos, brand marks or watermarks anywhere; any second person; any wide or full-body framing; any visible feet.
