# Artifact Readiness

Apply only the criteria relevant to the artifact's role.

- **Storyboard:** sequence understandable, shot purpose clear, placement coherent, no material continuity contradiction, ready for shot planning.
- **Reference frame:** composition resolved, approved references preserved, identity/product fidelity sufficient, usable as a shot target.
- **Motion prototype:** action, camera and timing testable; no gross motion defect invalidates the concept.
- **Video shot:** intended action and purpose achieved, technically usable, continuity sufficient, editorially usable.
- **Edit:** sequence complete, message/story understandable against supplied intent, pacing coherent, no obvious missing coverage.
- **Master:** approved edit represented, required audio/finishing present, technical QC passed.

A draft is ready when it can support its current decision; it does not need final polish.
