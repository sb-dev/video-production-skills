# Aster

**Level 5 — electric motorcycle campaign**

A multi-video launch package for a premium electric motorcycle with shared product references.

## Prompt

```text
Use video-production to direct the video production for a fictional electric motorcycle launch called "Aster".

This is a multi-video campaign, not one long video.

Where upstream Advertising, Narrative, or Music artifacts are provided, consume them as approved inputs. Do not silently rewrite campaign positioning, approved claims, narrative intent, or the internal music master.

Create a coherent video package containing:

1. Hero film — 45 seconds
2. Product film — 20 seconds
3. Social teaser — 10–15 seconds
4. 6-second bumper
5. 9:16 adaptations where appropriate

Brand/product:
- premium electric motorcycle
- sculpted black bodywork
- distinctive horizontal LED headlight
- exposed aluminium structural details
- minimal futuristic instrumentation

Campaign tone:
precision, quiet power, night-city confidence

Production should combine:
- product beauty shots
- riding footage
- engineering details
- environmental/lifestyle imagery
- restrained graphics
- supplied campaign messaging
- supplied or separately produced music

Workflow:
- Inspect upstream campaign, narrative, product, and music artifacts
- Build shared video visual direction
- Create persistent product references and product manifest
- Determine which shots/assets can be shared across productions
- Plan the hero film first
- Produce and approve reusable references before expensive motion generation
- Build the hero film through storyboard, shot plan, generation, edit, evaluation, and finishing
- Derive the product film from approved campaign/product material where appropriate without simply cutting the hero film blindly
- Produce teaser and bumper deliberately for their durations and channels
- Create delivery variants from approved masters
- Evaluate each production against its specific purpose
- Run technical QC on every deliverable

Preserve:
- motorcycle geometry
- lighting signature
- campaign art direction
- approved claims
- typography
- audio identity

Optimise for:
- cross-video consistency
- premium product fidelity
- efficient asset reuse
- channel-appropriate editing
- clear separation between master creative and delivery variants
```
