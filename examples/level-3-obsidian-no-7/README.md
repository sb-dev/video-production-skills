# Obsidian No. 7

**Level 3 — luxury fragrance**

A black-glass perfume bottle stays identical across macro, reveal, lifestyle, and hero shots.

## Prompt

```text
Use video-production to create a 15-second luxury fragrance commercial for a fictional perfume called "Obsidian No. 7".

The bottle must remain recognisably identical throughout the production.

Requirements:
- Duration: 15 seconds
- Master format: 16:9
- Delivery variants: 9:16 and 1:1
- Tone: dark, sensual, architectural, premium
- Product: rectangular black-glass perfume bottle with a brushed-metal cap
- Label: "OBSIDIAN No. 7"
- Palette: black, charcoal, deep amber highlights
- Audio: restrained atmospheric music, subtle glass/liquid sound design
- Final frame: product name and bottle hero composition

Sequence should include:
- macro material/detail shot
- controlled bottle reveal
- one atmospheric lifestyle or environmental shot
- final product hero shot

Workflow:
- Establish product manifest and non-negotiable visual attributes
- Define visual direction
- Storyboard the commercial
- Create shot plan and product-critical reference frames
- Generate and select each shot
- Assemble the edit
- Add supplied or generated audio where appropriate
- Create the program mix
- Finish a 16:9 master
- Derive 9:16 and 1:1 delivery variants from the approved creative
- Evaluate product fidelity separately from general visual quality
- Run technical QC on each deliverable

Do not allow the bottle geometry, label, cap, colour, or material character to drift between shots.

Optimise for:
- product fidelity
- glass and reflection quality
- premium lighting
- elegant pacing
- strong final product reveal
```
